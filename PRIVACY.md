# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 25, 2026_

SweetFin does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Your Jellyfin server

SweetFin connects only to the Jellyfin server(s) you add. Your credentials, settings and downloads stay on your device (credentials in the iOS Keychain). What your server does with your data is up to its administrator.

## Where your data lives and how to delete it

- **On your server**: watch history, ratings, watchlist and follows are stored by your Jellyfin server (or by the EnhancedFin plugin, if it is installed), not by SweetFin. You can remove them from the app (mark as unwatched, remove a rating, remove from the watchlist, unfollow) or ask your server administrator.
- **On your device**: downloads, settings and saved sign-ins stay on your device until you delete them in the app or delete the app.

## Third-party services

If your server runs the EnhancedFin plugin, some posters and backdrops are loaded directly from The Movie Database (TMDB), which receives your IP address like any website does. See the [TMDB privacy policy](https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy).

If you are a server administrator, the image editor also shows artwork that your server's metadata providers suggest. Those images are loaded from the addresses your server returns.
