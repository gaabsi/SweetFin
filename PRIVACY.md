# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2, 2026_

SweetFin does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Your Jellyfin server

SweetFin connects only to the Jellyfin server(s) you add. Your credentials, settings and downloads stay on your device (credentials in the iOS Keychain). What your server does with your data is up to its administrator.

## Third-party services

If your server runs the EnhancedFin plugin, some posters and backdrops are loaded directly from The Movie Database (TMDB), which receives your IP address like any website does. See the [TMDB privacy policy](https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy).
