SweetFin is a client for Jellyfin, the free and open-source media server you run on your own hardware.

SweetFin does not provide, host, sell or link to any media. It only plays what your own Jellyfin server provides, and you are responsible for the content on your server. A Jellyfin server is required.

FEATURES
• Direct play, no transcoding: ideal for small home servers
• Offline viewing: save movies and episodes from your own server to your device, when your server administrator allows it
• Skip intros, recaps and credits, then jump to the next episode
• Watch together with SyncPlay
• Clean audio and subtitle lists, grouped by language
• A redesigned home screen with a featured carousel and Continue Watching
• Designed for iPhone and iPad
• Default, Purple and Dark themes

KEEP TRACK OF WHAT YOU WATCH
With the free EnhancedFin plugin installed on your server, SweetFin brings your viewing history together:
• Details for movies and shows: synopsis, cast, ratings and release dates
• Rate what you have watched and keep a watchlist
• Follow movies and shows and see upcoming releases in a calendar
This information comes from TMDB and is retrieved by the plugin on your own server. Titles that are not on your server are shown for information only: they cannot be played or saved.

SweetFin is open source (MPL-2.0) and collects no data.
SweetFin is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Jellyfin project.
