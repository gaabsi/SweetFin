SweetFin is a client for Jellyfin, the free and open-source media server you run at home.

SweetFin does not provide, host or sell any media. It only plays what your own Jellyfin server provides, and you are responsible for the content on your server. A Jellyfin server is required.

FEATURES
• Direct play, no transcoding: ideal for small home servers
• Download movies and episodes to watch offline
• Skip intros, recaps and credits, then jump to the next episode
• Watch together with SyncPlay
• Clean audio and subtitle lists, grouped by language
• A redesigned home screen with a featured carousel and Continue Watching
• Designed for iPhone and iPad
• Default, Purple and Dark themes

KEEP TRACK OF EVERYTHING YOU WATCH
With the free EnhancedFin plugin on your server, SweetFin brings all your viewing in one place:
• Explore movies and shows: synopsis, cast, ratings and release dates
• Rate what you have watched and keep a watchlist
• Follow titles and see upcoming releases in a calendar
• SyncPlay invitations sent to another user
These features display information about titles from TMDB. Titles that are not on your server can be tracked, never played.

SweetFin is open source (MPL-2.0) and collects no data.
SweetFin is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Jellyfin project.
